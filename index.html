Pinxian Bie

I’m a PhD student in mathematics working in birational geometry and the theory of moduli spaces.

I’m interested in general theorems in arbitrary dimension and in the fine birational geometry of low-dimensional varieties. I’m drawn to explicit boundedness problems, especially for Fano varieties, where I study how bounds on anticanonical volumes depend on dimension and singularities. For threefolds of general type, how small can the canonical volume be? Which threefolds attain the minimum, and how do they vary in families?

I’d also like to explore number theory, complex geometry, and differential geometry. One path I’m considering is working in industry, perhaps in quantitative finance, and pursuing mathematical research independently in my spare time.

The Minimum Canonical Volume of Threefolds of General Type

Pinxian Bie, Peien Du, Zhengjie Yu

We prove that the minimum canonical volume among smooth complex projective threefolds of general type is 1/420. To exclude smaller volumes, we combine numerical constraints on singularities with the geometry of pluricanonical divisors and elliptic K3 surfaces.

arXiv:2611.12786

The Moduli of Canonical Threefolds of Minimum Volume

Pinxian Bie, Peien Du, Zhengjie Yu

We classify the canonical models attaining volume 1/420. The degree-46 weighted hypersurfaces form an irreducible rational component of dimension 33. Two further threefolds, Vega and Altair, give isolated points of the moduli space. We construct both and prove their rigidity.

arXiv:2611.18357

Sharp Polynomial Upper Bounds for Anticanonical Volumes

Pinxian Bie, Peien Du, Zhengjie Yu

In dimension n, we bound the anticanonical volume of a variety of ε-Fano type by a polynomial in 1/ε of degree 2n − n − 1, with a constant depending only on the dimension. Toric Fano varieties of Picard number one show that the exponent is optimal for every n ≥ 2.

arXiv:2610.10537
