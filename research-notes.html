Research Notes

I’m sharing these unsigned drafts to show what current AI systems can contribute to mathematical research when people work with them. Verification is still in progress. I’m keeping these versions off arXiv while I check the arguments, and I’ll update the notes as that work proceeds.

I welcome the responsible use of AI in mathematical research. I want to explore difficult problems with AI and to spend time reading AI-generated manuscripts closely, working through the proofs, and rewriting the arguments from my own perspective as a mathematician. In my view, people who use AI responsibly, understand such a paper in full, and can explain its mathematics independently deserve respect and a share of the credit for their contribution.

Posting a draft here makes no claim of priority or exclusive credit, and does not waive credit for the work. I welcome corrections and independent checks.

Extremal Iitaka volumes and pluricanonical pencils of threefolds over a curve

Completed 20 November 2026Human–AI collaboration

For complex projective threefolds with canonical singularities and Kodaira dimension one, the draft identifies 1/230 and 1/216 as the two smallest Iitaka volumes. It gives 230 as the largest possible first degree inducing the Iitaka fibration of an individual threefold, and 240 as the least single degree that works for all of them.

The two smallest Iitaka volumes and pluricanonical stability over a surface

Completed 20 November 2026Human–AI collaboration

In Kodaira dimension two, this draft proposes 1/600 and 1/520 as the smallest two Iitaka volumes of complex projective threefolds with canonical singularities. It gives examples at both values and argues that |mKX| induces the Iitaka fibration for every m ≥ 52. The minimum-volume example fails in degree 51.

The sharp Noether inequality for smooth minimal fourfolds

Completed 16 November 2026Human–AI collaboration

For a smooth complex projective fourfold with nef and big canonical divisor, the proposed inequality is 25KX4≥39pg(X)−244. The draft constructs examples on the proposed Noether line for infinitely many values of the geometric genus, which would make both coefficients sharp.
