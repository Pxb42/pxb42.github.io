The Minimum Canonical Volume of Threefolds of General Type

Pinxian Bie, Peien Du, Zhengjie Yu

We prove that the minimum canonical volume among smooth complex projective threefolds of general type is 1/420. To exclude smaller volumes, we combine numerical constraints on singularities with the geometry of pluricanonical divisors and elliptic K3 surfaces.

arXiv:2611.12786

The Moduli of Canonical Threefolds of Minimum Volume

Pinxian Bie, Peien Du, Zhengjie Yu

We classify the canonical models attaining volume 1/420. The degree-46 weighted hypersurfaces form an irreducible rational component of dimension 33. Two further threefolds, Vega and Altair, give isolated points of the moduli space. We construct both and prove their rigidity.

arXiv:2611.18357

Sharp Polynomial Upper Bounds for Anticanonical Volumes

Pinxian Bie, Peien Du, Zhengjie Yu

In dimension n, we bound the anticanonical volume of a variety of ε-Fano type by a polynomial in 1/ε of degree 2n − n − 1, with a constant depending only on the dimension. Toric Fano varieties of Picard number one show that the exponent is optimal for every n ≥ 2.

arXiv:2610.10537

Sharp anticanonical volume bounds for Gorenstein Fano fourfold of Picard number one

Pinxian Bie, Zhengjie Yu

For complex ℚ-factorial Gorenstein Fano fourfolds of Picard number one, we determine the largest anticanonical volume. It is 3528 for canonical singularities and 648 for terminal singularities. In the two cases the unique extremizers are ℙ(1, 1, 12, 28, 42) and ℙ(1, 1, 1, 1, 2), respectively.

arXiv:2610.09419

Sharp Quadratic Bounds for Anticanonical Volumes of Weak Fano Surfaces

Pinxian Bie

For every complex ε-lc weak del Pezzo surface, the anticanonical degree is at least cε2 for an explicit absolute constant c. The bound holds in every Picard number. Quotients of the projective plane show that this quadratic order is best possible as ε tends to zero.

arXiv:2609.06845

Construction of minimal varieties from quasi-smooth weighted complete intersections

Pinxian Bie

I give nefness criteria for the canonical divisor after weighted blow-ups of quasi-smooth weighted complete intersections. The construction produces 79 families of minimal threefolds of general type, infinite series in Kodaira dimension two, and 16 families on or near the Noether lines.
